## A named project outside the site

`playground-external/` sits next to the playground, not inside it, and is named
`external` in `astro.config.mjs`. Its own `likec4.config.json` declares
`"@": "./images"`, and its model writes `icon @/brand/logo.svg` — unchanged. The
icon is served from `/_astroc4/external/brand/logo.svg`.
